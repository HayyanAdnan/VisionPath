Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about VisionPath.

What is VisionPath?

VisionPath is Pakistan's #1 platform for discovering in-demand tech skills, finding the best learning resources, and connecting with top institutes — all completely free.

Is VisionPath really free?

Yes! 100% free forever. We believe every student in Pakistan deserves access to quality career guidance and learning resources without any cost.

How do I get my career roadmap?

Visit the Roadmap page, complete our AI-powered career assessment, and we'll generate a personalized roadmap with 5-6 career matches based on your interests, strengths, and goals.

How are skills ranked?

We use AI to scan thousands of job postings daily from LinkedIn, Rozee.pk, and other platforms. Skills are ranked by real-time demand, growth rate, and salary potential.

How do I find institutes near me?

Go to the Institutes section on our homepage. You'll find a list of verified IT institutes across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and other cities with addresses and course details.

How do I contact support?

You can email us at support@visionpath.pk, use WhatsApp, or fill out the contact form below. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Can I suggest a skill or resource?

Absolutely! We love community feedback. Use the contact form below to suggest skills, YouTube channels, platforms, or institutes you think should be added.

How do I reset my career selection?

On your Dashboard, scroll to the bottom and click the "Reset Career" button. This will clear your career selection so you can re-take the assessment.
